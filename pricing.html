How usage is counted

Nothing is metered until you choose it.

Pages count when extraction begins

Adding a file does not use processing allowance. The page count is shown before extraction starts.

Reports count when generation begins

One report is used when you choose to generate a comprehensive or focused report.

Patients are not the meter

Planned pricing is based on processing and reporting volume, not the number of patient records.

Nothing renews by surprise

Current allowance, overage and renewal terms are confirmed before a subscription is agreed.
