Before it reaches you

Three steps before the report is shared.

Your doctor stays responsible for review and clinical decisions.

01

Your doctor adds the records

Only the records under review go into the workspace.

02

Your doctor checks the draft

n1 organises recorded information. Your doctor checks the source pages, edits the wording, and decides what matters.

03

Your doctor shares one version

You receive the approved report chosen for you, to read and discuss together.

Sharing and privacy

Only the approved report is shared, not the clinical workspace.

SelectionYour doctor chooses which report to share.

AccessA report link can be password-protected, given an expiry, and revoked.

ReviewYour doctor can check the source page before sharing.
